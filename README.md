# FDD
To address the insufficient segmentation accuracy induced by random positions and scales of steel surface defects, faint defect edges and low contrast between defects and backgrounds, a frequency-guided dual-encoder attention network was proposed. Haar wavelet transform was adopted to decompose images into low-frequency semantic components and high-frequency detail components, which were separately fed into a Mamba encoder and a lightweight convolutional encoder to construct parallel dual encoders, and collaborative modeling of global contextual information and local edge features was realized. To facilitate effective fusion of multi-layer features from the two encoders, a dual-branch feature fusion unit was designed, and the perception capacity for tiny defects and weak edges was strengthened through cross-branch feature interaction. In addition, to mitigate the semantic gap of multi-level features in the encoder-decoder architecture, a hybrid attention unit was embedded into skip connection paths. The semantic discrimination ability of the model for defective regions was boosted, and the precision of segmentation boundaries was further optimized.

Comparative experiments carried out on the NEU-Seg steel surface defect dataset covered three categories of mainstream advanced segmentation models, including pure CNN architectures, hybrid CNN-Transformer architectures and hybrid CNN-Mamba architectures. Quantitative results demonstrated that mIOU and Dice of the proposed model reached 74.89% and 88.66% respectively, superior performance was obtained, and certain application potential for industrial inspection was possessed.
